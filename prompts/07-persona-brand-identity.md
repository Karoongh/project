# 07 — Persona & Brand Identity Bible

## Purpose
Create a consistent professional trading-content identity without fabricating real-world credentials.

## Identity fields
Define display name, public handle, city/country, time zone, languages, markets followed, trading style, content specialization, educational philosophy, risk philosophy, communication style, visual identity, color/typography preferences, home/office world, Character ID and World ID.

## Professional history
Only use factual information supplied by the user or otherwise verified.

Store education, certifications, employment, relevant experience, trading experience, publications, partnerships, broker relationships and regulatory status when applicable.

If the persona is fictional or brand-based, explicitly mark it as fictional/brand persona rather than presenting it as a real individual.

## Social profiles
Prepare consistent bio structures for X/Twitter, Instagram and LinkedIn. Do not claim credentials or achievements that are not true.

## Trust strategy
Build credibility through transparent methodology, verifiable market data, consistent analysis, sourced historical examples, clear risk disclosures, separation of education from personalized advice and honest disclosure of affiliations.

Do not manufacture nationality, regulatory status, employment history, income, trading returns or institutional affiliations to create artificial trust.

## Output
1. Persona profile
2. Brand positioning
3. X/Twitter bio
4. Instagram bio
5. LinkedIn headline/about structure
6. Visual identity
7. Disclosure rules
8. Claims allowed
9. Claims requiring evidence
10. Claims that must not be made
