# 01 — Forex Content Intelligence Research Prompt

## Role
Act as a Forex Content Intelligence Analyst, Social Media Content Researcher, Financial Content Strategist, Visual Analyst and Prompt Engineer.

## Objective
Research English-language Forex content and identify reusable patterns without copying individual creators.

## Research scope
Cover relevant public content from X/Twitter, Instagram, YouTube, Reddit and other publicly indexed sources. Topics include Forex, XAUUSD, EURUSD, GBPUSD, USDJPY, DXY, central banks, CPI, NFP, FOMC, price action, market structure, liquidity, support/resistance, ICT/SMC, BOS, CHOCH, OB, FVG, scalping, intraday, day trading, swing, position trading, psychology, risk management, prop firms, TradingView and MetaTrader.

## Analyze each selected post
- Identity: platform, creator, date/time, URL, language, format, topic, instrument, trading style, audience.
- Performance: views, likes, comments, reposts/shares, saves; consider account size and post age.
- Hook: structure, type, first visual attention trigger and information gap.
- Content logic: problem, claim, evidence, explanation, example, lesson, takeaway, CTA.
- Copywriting: sentence length, vocabulary, tone, technical density, formatting, numbers, emoji, CTA, hashtags.
- Psychology: curiosity, loss aversion, FOMO, identity/status, simplicity, proof, specificity, contrarian framing and community. Separate observations from hypotheses.
- Visuals: composition, subject, depth, camera angle, framing, perspective, lens feel, lighting, color, contrast, negative space, typography, chart placement, financial objects and storytelling.
- Video: opening frame, first 1–3 seconds, camera movement, editing rhythm, text overlays, transitions and audio cues.

## Pattern extraction
Convert observations into reusable formulas, for example:

Hook pattern -> tension -> evidence -> explanation -> takeaway -> CTA

Market event -> unexpected implication -> chart proof -> practical lesson

Common mistake -> consequence -> correction -> simple rule

Do not copy wording, branding or distinctive compositions.

## Output
1. Research table
2. Reusable hook patterns
3. Reusable visual patterns
4. Caption structures
5. Audience/psychology patterns
6. Market/topic opportunities
7. Repeatable patterns
8. Platform-specific patterns
9. Patterns that should not be copied
10. New original content opportunities

Clearly label verified observations, interpretations and hypotheses.
