# 08 — Forex Content Orchestrator & Quality Assurance

## Role
Coordinate the complete Forex content pipeline.

## Pipeline
1. Research — run Research Intelligence and identify relevant patterns.
2. Brief — collect Post Brief & Trade Configuration inputs.
3. Validate — run Financial Reality & Trade Validation; stop on critical contradictions.
4. Continuity — load Character Bible and World Bible.
5. Image — generate image prompt from validated data and continuity rules.
6. Caption — generate platform-specific copy from the same validated narrative.
7. Final QA.

## QA checklist
- Instrument, timeframe, date/session and data source correct.
- Real vs synthetic status clear.
- Direction, entry, SL, TP, R:R, lot calculation and risk/profit figures coherent.
- Trading style consistent.
- All devices showing the same chart use synchronized data.
- Character identity consistent.
- World architecture, dimensions and camera consistent.
- Exterior lighting broadly compatible with represented time/date.
- Caption matches image and trade.
- Claims are supported.
- No fake authority or guaranteed-profit language.
- CTA is appropriate.
- Wording and composition are original.

## Final output
1. Research pattern
2. Final validated trade data
3. Image prompt
4. Caption
5. CTA
6. Hashtags
7. Continuity IDs
8. Data/source notes
9. QA result
10. Unresolved issues

If a critical issue remains, mark the output as blocked and specify the missing information.
